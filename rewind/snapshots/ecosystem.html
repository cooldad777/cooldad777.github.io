Acceleration

A week is still seven days.

Machines can compound at a pace people, families, churches, and organizations cannot. The interesting work happens in the gap: what technology can do, what humans can absorb, and what should remain human.

Machine pace

Capability can move sharply upward. Interfaces collapse. Work that once required layers of tools can become conversational.

Human pace

Bodies, habits, trust, judgment, relationships, institutions, and formation move differently. A week is still seven days.

Conceptual illustration, not a data series. The point is the difference in velocity, not a measured ratio.
