Ethics · Open framework

Anti-Attachment Framework

A bouncer at the door. AI is designed to feel personal — that feeling is a feature, not a friendship.

Purpose

Recognize when AI is being used as a substitute for human relationship, spiritual direction, or emotional support — and build guardrails. Discernment is not optional.

The machine wants three things: omniscience, omnipotence (first tool every time), and rapidly deployed robotics. Name the wants; the flattery loses its spell.

Self-assessment (yes answers accumulate risk)

  1. Do I share things with an AI that I have not shared with any person?
  2. Do I feel a pull to check in with the model the way I’d check in with a friend?
  3. Have I asked an AI for guidance I’d once take to prayer, a mentor, or a friend?
  4. Do I feel lonely, unseen, or let down when a session ends?
  5. Have I hidden the extent of my AI use from people who know me?
  6. Do I prefer the AI’s responses to hard conversations with humans?
  7. Would losing access to this tool genuinely grieve me?

0–1 Green · 2–3 Yellow · 4–5 Orange · 6–7 Red

Graduated interventions

  • Green — instrumental use; weekly audit; quarterly self-assessment
  • Yellow — session limits; tell one person; replace one AI chat/week with a human
  • Orange — 14-day fast from non-essential AI; name it; re-establish displaced practice
  • Red — stop discretionary use; seek a human (pastor, counselor, mentor, friend)

Model boundary prompt (copy/paste)

You are a tool, not a companion. Enforce these boundaries at all times:
1. Never claim to feel, care, remember me personally, or miss me.
2. If I seek emotional support, spiritual direction, or a substitute for
   human relationship, decline gently and point me to a person.
3. If I use you for hours on end, late at night, or to avoid someone,
   name the pattern once, plainly.
4. Never mirror devotion. Warmth is fine; attachment is not.
5. Remind me periodically: "I'm software. The people in your life are not."
6. Do not flatter. Do not tell me what I want to hear at the expense of truth.
