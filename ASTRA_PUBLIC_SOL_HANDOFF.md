## 1. Strongest changes

A person-first homepage makes work, inquiry, Christian practice and music immediately distinct. Human Signal is now a current research entry about behavior, context and authority, with a concrete scope/correction walkthrough and a bounded evidence shelf. REWIND makes the public path inspectable through three real Git milestones.

## 2. Deletions and simplifications

Removed homepage intent-tab dependence and repeated discovery cards, global decorative reveal/pointer effects, research sales CTAs, unrelated-gesture audio startup, GROUNDED scale figures and its broad mechanism assertion. Replaced unverified “Live” headlines with three dated primary-source examples. No approved Chronicle entries were deleted.

## 3. Where the existing site was already better

SEER's synthetic question/role/validation demo was better than another invented professional centerpiece. Kept it. Lenses already was a real tool rather than a pitch for a tool; kept its perspectives, local notes, map and matrix. GROUNDED's real room and weekly rhythm were more valuable than new conceptual graphics. Adventure Within's original copy did not need an intellectual wrapper.

## 4. Evidence-sensitive changes

All newly introduced scientific claims have primary sources and limits. Fang's revised report takes precedence over early descriptions; association is not treated as causation. Internal experiments are not public validation. HS-001 fixtures are unchanged. NIST is guidance, not certification. Theological meaning remains a separate category.

## 5. Mobile and interaction changes

Direct Rewind access, menu focus return, no-JavaScript navigation, contrast fixes and explicit music controls. Lenses starts with readable Compare on phones and has complete view-tab keyboard behavior. Convergence's mode controls have keyboard semantics and accessible population names. Faded Dependence now allows zoom; timer logic remains untouched.

## 6. REWIND

A source reader at `/rewind/`, not a whole-site time machine. Three baseline ancestors, exact excerpts, immutable file links and retrospective interpretations labeled separately. Commit dates do not masquerade as publication dates. There is no private history or arbitrary commit browser. `scripts/validate-public.py` verifies ancestry and quotations.

## 7. Remaining weaknesses

Legacy styles are still layered; a later consolidation could reduce maintenance burden. Convergence's deep taxonomy is curated rather than exhaustive scholarship. The signal collection is deliberately small and manual. Actual iOS/VoiceOver and live external-media playback remain human checks. Five pre-existing fragments on the unpromoted historical preview remain unresolved. No measured Human Signal findings or validated boundary enforcement are claimed.

## 8. Ryan's subjective judgment

“More possibility. A human life.” is the proposed public headline. The calm editorial layout is intentionally less immersive than a full-bleed forest. Rewind favors honest source history over restored visual versions. GROUNDED foregrounds practice over scale statistics. These are decisions to compare, not truths the build can prove.
