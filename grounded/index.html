GROUNDED

Refuge from the tendrils. One hour. Phone in a box.

Kurzweil said this century would feel like twenty thousand years of progress. He offered it as a forecast. Treat it as a warning. No nervous system was built for that speed. GROUNDED is for men who want a room the feed cannot enter.

Not a brand. Not woods-as-identity. The trees in the photograph are a place to stand, not a costume. Not a Bible-study product. Not a detox program. Training in attention, with other men, in a room.

“Your body is the primary place of your spiritual life. If you do not meet God in your body, in the concrete places where your feet stand, you will not meet Him anywhere.”Dallas Willard · The Divine Conspiracy

Sixty minutes. Phones in a lockbox. A beeper on the table so a family can still reach you. One short passage of Scripture, read slowly. Silence. One question. One concrete thing for the week, asked about the next time.

01 · Arrive

Phones in the lockbox. Lid closed. Beeper on the table for emergencies only.

02 · Listen

One passage, read aloud. Four slow passes if you want a name: read, reflect, respond, rest. Mostly you sit in the silence you forgot existed.

03 · Share

“What did you hear?” No commentary. No fixing. No one gets to win the room.

04 · Commit

One specific thing this week. Spoken out loud. Checked next week. That is the whole curriculum.

Two men is a group. Text one man you respect: “I’m keeping an hour with the phone in a box. Scripture and silence. You in?”
