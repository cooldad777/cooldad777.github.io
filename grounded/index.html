GROUNDED

An hour a week with the phone put away.

Kurzweil said this century would feel like twenty thousand years of progress. The feed already behaves that way. A week does not. GROUNDED is practice on the clock that does not double.

It is not a brand. It is not woods-as-identity. It is not a Bible-study product and it is not a detox program. It is training in attention, with other people, in a room.

“Your body is the primary place of your spiritual life. If you do not meet God in your body, in the concrete places where your feet stand, you will not meet Him anywhere.”Dallas Willard · The Divine Conspiracy

Every week: sixty minutes. Phones in a lockbox. A beeper on the table so a family can still reach you. One short passage of Scripture, read slowly. Silence. Then one question. Then one concrete thing for the week, asked about the next time.

1 · Arrive

Phones in the lockbox. Lid closed. Beeper on the table for emergencies only.

2 · Listen

One passage, read aloud. Four slow passes if you want a name for it: read, reflect, respond, rest. Mostly you sit in the silence you forgot existed.

3 · Share

“What did you hear?” No commentary. No fixing. No one gets to win the room.

4 · Commit

One specific thing this week. Spoken out loud. Checked next week. That is the whole curriculum.

Two people is a group. Text one person you respect: “I’m keeping an hour with the phone in a box. Scripture and silence. You in?”

Kennesaw, Georgia. Not a store. An hour.
