GROUNDED

Men gather. Followers of Jesus. Arrive · Listen · Share · Commit.

“Your body is the primary place of your spiritual life. If you do not meet God in your body, in the concrete places where your feet stand, you will not meet Him anywhere.”Dallas Willard · The Divine Conspiracy
“The whole point of seeing through something is to see something through it. To ‘see through’ all things is the same as not to see.”C. S. Lewis · The Abolition of Man

Time to lean on each other — take back some ground, and make space for the Holy Spirit to give us hope and self-control. Hope for us, and for those who watch us.

A small Bible-study component within Christian spiritual formation, personal lives, and learning how to live as a flourishing Christian with exponential tech. Talking about it, putting it in the open, is a catalyst. Best practices, worked out together. A community, because alone you cannot fight the algorithms.

Not a brand. Not woods-as-identity. Not a classic full-hour Bible study. Not a tech-detox program. Training in attention, with other people, in a room.

Arrive

Phones in the lockbox. Beeper on the table.

Listen

One passage read aloud. Silence together.

Share

“What did you hear?” No commentary. No fixing.

Commit

One thing this week. Checked next week.
