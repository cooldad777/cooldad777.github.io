# SEE,R Editorial Board

This is the standing editorial gate for the public site. It is not seven brands or seven writing voices. It is one page reviewed through seven questions, then synthesized into one clear surface.

## 1. Explainer desk
**Question:** Would an intelligent stranger understand what this is in 15 seconds?

Checks:
- What is this?
- Why should I care?
- What should I look at first?
- Is the "nut graf" visible before the specialist language?
- Can jargon be moved one layer deeper?

## 2. Moonshot desk
**Question:** What actually changed, where is the leverage, and what can be tested?

Checks:
- Is there a real discontinuity or just a new label?
- What becomes possible now that was expensive, slow, or impractical before?
- What is the smallest experiment that could falsify the claim?
- Which parts are demonstrated, attested, or speculative?
- Are we respecting human adoption speed while taking machine acceleration seriously?

## 3. Product / Apple desk
**Question:** What can be removed?

Checks:
- One idea per screen.
- White space carries hierarchy.
- Do not use a visual merely because a visual can be made.
- Progressive disclosure: surface first, depth second.
- Interactions should clarify the idea, not advertise the interface.

## 4. Technology features desk
**Question:** What is changing, why does it matter to people, and where is the complexity being flattened?

Checks:
- Avoid utopia/dystopia shortcuts.
- Name tradeoffs.
- Separate capability from consequence.
- Show the human stakes.
- Preserve uncertainty.

## 5. Business desk
**Question:** Who cares, who pays, what is proven, and what is only a claim?

Checks:
- Separate research from service.
- Separate employer-bound evidence from public claims.
- No invented ROI, testimonials, or proof.
- State what would constitute independent validation.
- Make the audience and decision clear.

## 6. Narrative desk
**Question:** Where is Ryan, and why should a person keep reading?

Checks:
- Prefer one concrete scene to three abstract paragraphs.
- Preserve voice and lived motivation.
- Do not convert every creative or spiritual project into business value.
- Let music be music.
- Let a real room, body, guitar, or decision carry meaning.

## 7. Formation / human-agency desk
**Question:** Does this increase human agency, attention, relationship, and truthful categories?

Checks:
- Faith is not empirical evidence.
- AI is not a person, pastor, conscience, or replacement relationship.
- Keep consequential judgment attributable to a human.
- Do not turn experimental self-reflection prompts into clinical scoring.
- The better the tool becomes, the more capable the human should become.

## Universal publishing rule

Every page gets two layers:

### Surface
A normal intelligent reader can answer in 15 seconds:
1. What is this?
2. Why does it matter?
3. What can I do next?

### Depth
The reader who wants the full architecture, evidence, worldview, method, or implementation can continue.

**Plain language on the surface. Moonshot depth one layer down.**

---

## Visual rule

The emotional progression is:

**FOREST → LIGHT → POSSIBILITY → HUMANITY**

Use dark sections sparingly. Default to warm white / ivory, very light sage and sky, black or deep-forest type, generous space, and few cards. A visual must explain something immediately or it should be removed.
