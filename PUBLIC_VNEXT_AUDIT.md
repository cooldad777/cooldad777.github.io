# PUBLIC vNEXT — INITIAL AUDIT

Status: WORKING NOTES FOR PUBLIC BRANCH
Date: 2026-10-07

## What is already strong

- The homepage has a memorable nature-forward identity and a clear Work / Explore fork.
- SEER Solutions already starts with decisions rather than tools and keeps confidential work out of public evidence.
- Lenses has the right epistemic instinct: perspectives remain separate and source boundaries stay visible.
- GROUNDED is concrete, embodied, communal, and sourced.
- Adventure Within is properly separate from professional identity.
- Chronicle already moved toward curated public decisions rather than a raw private log.
- Reduced-motion support and accessible interaction patterns already exist in the shared layer.

## Where the public ecosystem lags the current thinking

1. The umbrella thesis still sounds mostly like “AI expands possibility.” It should now include authority, boundaries, continuity, and limits.
2. Human Signal is framed mainly around model behavior and portable boundaries. It should add the harder question: what becomes durable, what crosses scopes, and who authorizes consequential changes.
3. Anti-Attachment is useful but should be more explicit about the evidence boundary between attachment research, reflection prompts, and product claims.
4. Governance language on SEER Solutions can be sharper: scope before retrieval; evidence before release; consequential authority outside model confidence; meaningful revision/receipt.
5. Public Chronicle and the current navigation do not yet express REWIND as a first-class idea.
6. Some surfaces expose internal taxonomy or operational history that belongs backstage; AI Tracker should remain secondary/backstage rather than a primary public door.
7. The visual family is coherent but still card-heavy. vNext should use more spatial hierarchy, scroll-linked transitions, and fewer simultaneous destinations.
8. Interactive controls should answer a visitor question, not merely demonstrate interaction.

## Public evidence posture

Good public sources for the refined research story include:
- NIST AI RMF / Generative AI Profile for risk-management framing.
- Sharma et al., ICLR 2024, for measured sycophancy in RLHF-trained assistants.
- PersistBench, ICML 2026, for cross-domain leakage and memory-induced sycophancy in long-term-memory settings.
- 2026 Nature Human Behaviour work on AI-companion use / attachment-related outcomes.

Use these as evidence for the existence of risks and open research questions, not as proof that S33R's internal architecture is correct.
