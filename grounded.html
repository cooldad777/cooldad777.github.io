GROUNDED

A weekly practice for men who are done being consumed by the feed. Not a Bible study. Not a tech-detox product. Training in attention.

Every week, a small band of men gathers for ~60 minutes. Phones go in a lockbox. A beeper sits on the table — emergencies only, so family can reach us. One passage of Scripture read aloud. Silence. Share what we heard. Commit to one specific, measurable step to reclaim attention.

No screens. No sermons. No curriculum. Just men, a text, and silence.

1. Arrive
Phones in the lockbox.
2. Listen
One passage. Silence.
3. Share
“What did you hear?”
4. Commit
One measurable step.

Our sons are watching. Our daughters are watching. The algorithm is not raising them. We are.
