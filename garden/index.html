Graduation rules

Graduate-to-public: mature notes earn a URL; paid and private stay back. A note clears the gate when it is:

  • Public-safe — Settled / public-safe only
  • No employer — no employer facts, client IDs, or Never List
  • Dated — facts vs interpretation labeled; dates present
  • Stands alone — useful to a stranger or LLM by URL alone

Private novel catalog never on Pages.

Stages

  • Seed — Early public-safe note — planted in the nursery, not ready to graduate.
  • Sapling — Growing — more shape, still in the nursery.
  • Fruit — Mature enough to earn a public page. Fruit is a graduated note, not a home door.
