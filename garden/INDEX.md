## Purpose

Public-safe idea nursery. Seed → Sapling → Fruit. Not the Chronicle.

Ideas are planted here, labeled by maturity, tagged to zero or more projects, and graduated to public URLs when they clear the gate.

---

Seer Chronicle shows how thinking moved. Idea Garden is where ideas are planted and grown.

## Stages

| Stage | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Seed** | Early public-safe note — planted in the nursery, not ready to graduate. |
| **Sapling** | Growing — more shape, still in the nursery. |
| **Fruit** | Mature enough to earn a public page. Fruit is a graduated note, not a home door. |

Tag ideas with `projects[]` (0..n). Overlap is fine. Fruit ≠ home door.

---

## Graduation rules (light)

Graduate-to-public: mature notes earn a URL; paid and private stay back.

1. **Public-safe** — Settled / public-safe only  
2. **No employer** — no employer facts, client IDs, or Never List  
3. **Dated** — facts vs interpretation labeled; dates present  
4. **Stands alone** — useful to a stranger or LLM by URL alone  

Private novel catalog never on Pages. Monitor stays private.
