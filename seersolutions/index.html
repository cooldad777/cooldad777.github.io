The work is in the gap.

Technology moves quickly. Organizations absorb change at a different speed. The work is teaching the shop to use what it already has.

  1. New capabilities arrive faster than most operating models change.

  2. The useful question is rarely whether data exists. It is whether the right grain reaches the right decision.

  3. A model can be technically impressive and operationally useless.

  4. Value appears when the output enters a real workflow.

  5. Someone still has to trust it, use it, and own the outcome.

  6. The point is not the dashboard. The point is the decision.
