The workflow is the system around the AI.

Abstract sequence. Not a map of any organization’s system.

AI is easy to access. The hard part is making it operational.

AI becomes dramatically more useful when it is connected to the operating workflow rather than treated as a chat window.

An operating workflow, in the abstract

  1. Intake
  2. Triage
  3. Route
  4. Build / refresh / discover
  5. Validate
  6. Deliver
  7. Measure

A production AI workflow should have explicit intake, routing, scheduling, validation, and quarantine boundaries.

The boundary is the point. The mechanism is not on this page.

  3. Human decides.
  4. System records.

A boundary, not a description of a system.

About

Ryan See

Ryan See is the founder of SEER Solutions and an analytics leader with 15 years of experience turning data into decisions.

His work has spanned analytics, operations, healthcare data, and executive-facing reporting, with hands-on experience across Power BI, SQL, Azure, Tableau, Salesforce, and related business systems.

He works at the intersection of business questions, measurement, technology, and adoption: figuring out what should be measured, building the system that measures it, and helping the people who use it make decisions with it.
