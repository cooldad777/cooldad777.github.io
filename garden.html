How ideas move from private Pensieve to public URL — and what stays back.

Default policy

Graduate-to-public (safer than open-by-default). Mature notes earn a page; paid/sensitive stay local.

Graduate gate (working)

  1. No employer facts, client identifiers, or sacred/Never List content
  2. Facts labeled vs interpretation; dates present
  3. Fits a locked name from the naming frame
  4. Useful to a stranger or an LLM by URL alone
