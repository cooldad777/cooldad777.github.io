Lenses

How different lenses talk to the same future.

Short public-safe cards from the SEE, R Worldview Matrix. No tradition endorsed over another. Full matrix and talkable MD later.

Imagination · natural law

C. S. Lewis

Talks about moral order you don’t invent (the Tao), and about technique as power some people hold over others. Distraction and conditioning matter more than dramatic villainy. Useful when the conversation turns to formation, propaganda, or “neutral” tools.

Measured · contested

Scientific / empirical

Separates documented mechanism from projected harm. Framing effects, engagement design, and personalization are established; population-level magnitudes often contested. Useful when claims need receipts before doctrine or doom.

Stress-test · not endorsement

Moonshot Mates / acceleration

Pushes upside hard — build faster, ship the future, treat caution as friction — so weak claims break in daylight. Labeled as a stress-test lens on this site, not a creed. Useful when optimism needs an honest opponent.

Protestant · Reformed / evangelical

Reformed / evangelical

Reads attention and attachment as rival discipleship. Word and Spirit through ordinary means; suspicion of neutrality claims from fallen hands. Useful when the question is formation, grace, and what catechizes daily life.

Place · Marietta

Local Marietta stream

Listens from a local Protestant place — people and practice before abstract systems. Kept public-safe and quiet: no venue billboard, no caption energy on the music path. Useful when the future has to land in a real town.
