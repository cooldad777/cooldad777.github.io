Architecture · Public explanation

Digital Pensieve

Local, encrypted, self-owned archive turned into a queryable intelligence system — with a hard rule: three layers that must never blend.

Three-layer doctrine

LayerNameContentsRule
1Raw ArchiveBit-for-bit exports as receivedFrozen — never edited in place
2Factual TimelineDates, people, places, events extracted by scriptsRegenerable from Layer 1
3InterpretationThemes, patterns, inferences (AI or later reflection)Always marked: model, date, sources

An AI inference must never be mistaken for something you wrote. A reconstructed fact must never be mistaken for the original record.

Why it matters

  • Memory contamination — unlabeled summaries rewrite your past
  • Ledger corruption — forecasting proof needs original timestamped words
  • Consulting liability — clients must separate records from machine interpretation

Public non-goals

Not therapy. Not diagnosis. Not a companion. Local-first: we never possess your archive. See Seer and Anti-Attachment.
