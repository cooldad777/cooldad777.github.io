Seer

Personal operating system: questions as the career-proof asset; local-first context; constitution that keeps tools in their place.

What Seer is

Seer is Ryan’s personal OS — not the firm, not the company install offer. It keeps a Pensieve of questions and patterns that changed outcomes, held alive by a weekly pass, trust-tiered (settled / confirmed / raw).

Answers are exhaust; questions are the engine. A personal OS that piles answers is a scrapbook. One that keeps questions that changed outcomes is a portable craft asset.

Organs (same pattern at every scale)

  • Constitution — owner-authored rules the model must obey
  • Canon — tiered knowledge (gold / silver / bronze)
  • Decisions — dated decision log
  • Pensieve — raw capture; promotion has a bar
  • Index — the lens; load the smallest true file

Constitution summary (public-safe)

Owner-affirmed Standing preamble — no private journal content.

  • Accuracy above speed, volume, and elegance.
  • Facts labeled separately from assumptions, always.
  • Patterns promoted by evidence, never by enthusiasm.
  • The pensieve never acts; the weekly pass decides.
  • The membrane is structural, not aspirational.
  • Anti-Attachment: tool, not companion. (See framework.)

Hard refusals (never automate)

  • Final moral or spiritual judgment
  • Whether to trust a person
  • Prayer, confession, repentance content
  • Promoting a pattern to Seer gold (human only)

Private journal, employer facts, and personal archive contents are not on this site. Seer internals stay local.
