## vNext thesis

The public ecosystem should express a more mature idea:

**Use more intelligence. Keep the human authority, boundaries, history, and room for surprise.**

This is not a claim that one memory architecture, model, or AI provider is correct. It is the public-facing synthesis of the work so far.

## Design principles

- Apple-like restraint, not Apple imitation.
- Motion explains hierarchy or change; it is not decoration.
- Progressive disclosure before information dumping.
- Public truth over private cleverness.
- Science gets primary/credible sources and caveats.
- Internal synthetic experiments may guide what Ryan tests next; they are not presented as external proof.
- Theology may shape questions and values; it is not used as scientific evidence.
- Public/private, professional/personal, and project boundaries stay explicit.
- Reduced-motion behavior is first-class.
- Mobile should feel intentional, not like desktop collapsed.
