Working principles

What the work keeps teaching me.

See the research ↗
01

Scope before relevance.

Decide what a system may see before asking what it thinks is useful.

02

Preserve before adopting.

A captured thought can stay intact without quietly becoming a belief, preference, or decision.

03

Confidence is not authority.

Fluent output can inform a decision. It does not earn the right to make consequential choices by sounding certain.

04

Keep the path.

Useful systems can correct themselves without pretending the earlier state never existed.

These are working design principles, not laws of nature. Published research informs the questions; the public Human Signal pages keep evidence separate from interpretation.
