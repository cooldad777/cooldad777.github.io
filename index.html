Lenses

A core filter for every node

Seeing from more than one place is the point. When an idea lands — GROUNDED, a consulting claim, a music release, a Chronicle beat — run it through lenses you can adapt. Not an endorsement map. Exponential tech hits ancient categories (attention, attachment, hope, the end of things) at new scale; different voices name that collision differently. Bonus question under each: what might this work move for people and souls?

C. S. Lewis

Moral order you don’t invent (the Tao). “Man’s power over nature” is usually some people’s power over others through technique. Exponential tools industrialize distraction and conditioning — seriousness never gets a quiet minute. Work that keeps body, honesty, and formation visible (GROUNDED, Anti-Attachment, open Chronicle) aims at seeing through to something real — not explaining everything away.

Scientific / empirical

Documented mechanism first: framing, engagement design, personalization. Population-level harm and grand end-times forecasts stay contested until measured. Exponential delivery is already engineered; eschatology is not a lab result. Moving the ball here means marking fact vs forecast in public writing and client work — so hype and doom don’t catechize people in the dark.

Moonshot / acceleration

Stress-test Push the upside hard — build faster, ship capability, treat caution as friction — until weak claims crack. Secular eschatology often lives here (abundance, singularity, exit from scarcity). On this site it’s a pressure tool, not a creed. The ball moves when tools actually serve households and firms without eating the attention those futures need.

Reformed / evangelical

Word, grace, ordinary means. The feed is rival discipleship — attention and attachment catechize daily. Eschatology: history bends toward Christ’s return, so tech is neither savior nor side note; it’s a field where faithfulness or worldliness shows. GROUNDED and Anti-Attachment rehearse that locally: reclaim attention, refuse synthetic friendship, practice presence that forms souls for a kingdom models don’t build.

Local Kennesaw stream

Place and people before abstraction. Exponential tech lands in real homes, kids, churches, and Southeast firms — not a slide deck. Eschatology stays neighbor-shaped: who you’ll answer for on the ground. Moving the ball means one lockbox hour, one honest framework, one human engagement that works in Kennesaw — so souls aren’t abandoned to the feed while the future argues with itself.
