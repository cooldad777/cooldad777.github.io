The Convergence

Hope, ending, and exponential technology keep landing in the same room. This page is a way to look — not a chart that preaches.

Different traditions already have language for power that wants heaven, for attention that catechizes, for a world that feels near an edge. The work here is to see through those languages, side by side, without flattening them into one slogan. Analysis, not endorsement.
