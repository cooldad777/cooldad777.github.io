The Convergence

People already have language for the end of an age and for power that wants to reach heaven. Machine-scale technology lands in those old sentences. Tap a circle. Filter a tradition. This is study, not endorsement.

How to read this

Circle size follows rough world population for that family — so you can see what most of the world is standing in, not what a feed promotes.

Each card names two things that family already teaches: what happens at the edge of history, and what unrestrained human power looks like. I then note where exponential technology rhymes with that teaching. Rhyme is not proof.

Figures are rounded public estimates. Protestant here includes evangelical, mainline, and nondenominational streams. Reformed and Southern Baptist sit inside that Protestant circle until a later pass splits them.
