Why some branches are sized and others are not

Good maps know where the data stops.

Pew's 2025 global update gives a strong top-level picture for 2020, but it explicitly does not subdivide those global categories into denominations. The Christian branch proportions shown here therefore use Pew's older 2010 global Christian study. Below the broad Catholic / Protestant / Orthodox split, this becomes a taxonomy for orientation rather than a proportional census.

That is deliberate. A precise-looking number with a weak denominator would make the interface prettier and the thinking worse.
