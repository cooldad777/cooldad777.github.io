Read the sources · 8 starting points
Antiqua et nova · 2025 ↗Theological + ethical teaching

The Vatican’s note distinguishes human intelligence from AI and places human dignity and the common good at the center of its evaluation.

Qur’an 49:6 · read with tafsir ↗Scriptural reading

This passage warns against acting on unverified news in ways that harm others. The AI application here is an editorial question, not a ruling.

Pirkei Avot 1:14 ↗Rabbinic ethical text

Hillel’s questions bring responsibility to oneself, responsibility beyond oneself, and the urgency of action into view.

Bhagavad Gita 2:47 · translation and commentary ↗Hindu scriptural reading

This verse connects action with a warning against attachment to its fruits and against inaction.

Dhammapada · Chapter 20 ↗Buddhist scriptural reading

The path verses include reflection on the impermanence of conditioned things.

NIST AI Risk Management Framework ↗Technical governance framework

The voluntary AI RMF supports incorporating trustworthiness into AI design, use, and evaluation.

UNESCO · Recommendation on the Ethics of AI ↗International ethical recommendation

The recommendation addresses proportionality, safety, privacy, participation, accountability, and oversight.

Global Indigenous Data Alliance · CARE ↗People- and purpose-oriented principles

CARE foregrounds collective benefit, authority to control, responsibility, and ethics in Indigenous data governance.

A map with visible edges.

These are selected texts and frameworks, not rankings of religions or a census of belief. The questions applying them to AI are editorial interpretations. They do not speak on behalf of the authors or communities.

Scientific method and philosophical naturalism are not interchangeable. Religious meaning is not empirical validation. A technological resemblance to a sacred image does not establish prophetic fulfillment.

The wider research includes Christian eschatology and denominational differences, other religious and philosophical traditions, and forecasts about technology. Those branches require their own sources and context; this first comparison set does not claim to exhaust them.
