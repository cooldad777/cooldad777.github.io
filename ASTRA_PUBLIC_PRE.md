- Homepage: real forest photography, star identity, clear professional/personal doors, humane language, and music as music.

## Audit: what is weak
- Umbrella makes visitors pass several abstract sections and duplicate discovery surfaces. Intent tabs hide destinations and require JSON before showing them. Typography is attractive but many labels are tiny.
- Research homepage is tied to a single fictional comparison, says it “measures” behavior although nothing was run, and lacks durable context, correction, scope and continuity. Its booking emphasis blurs research and professional practice.
- Repeated card treatments, hover glass and hero motion compete without explaining a relationship. Multiple inherited CSS layers make maintenance fragile.
- Mobile navigation places all controls behind Menu; Escape handling does not explicitly restore focus. Some legacy pages lack skip links. Convergence tabs need complete keyboard semantics, and narrow population segments need accessible names.
- “Live” Convergence content is a dated static collection. Several current-event claims have not been independently verified in this pass. Do not carry them as confirmed fact merely because URLs look plausible.
- GROUNDED asserts shared attachment machinery more broadly than the cited commentary warrants. Commercial scale numbers are an unnecessary evidentiary burden for a formation practice.
- Existing public previews and experimental tools should not become new main-navigation destinations. Open Fruit exposes “Vault” terminology with no visitor benefit.
- Research method/scenario fixtures are valuable historical demonstrations, not public scientific validation. No internal research handoff is needed for this independent public pass.

## Proposed thesis and narrative spine
A life with more possibility—and room to choose what matters. Ryan's analytics, inquiry, Christian practice and music are understandable first. The research strand then asks: as tools become more capable and persistent, how do people retain authority, boundaries, continuity and the ability to correct them?

Capability and authority are different categories. Present this as a design commitment, not an empirical theorem. No natural mechanism or theological analogy prescribes this software architecture.

## Content strategy
Concrete nouns and visitor questions. No new client outcomes, employer implementation details, private history, personality assessments or ROI claims. Preserve approved Chronicle contents. Source new scientific claims directly. Update descriptive metadata to match edited content. Avoid turning principles into a manifesto on every page.

## UX and interaction strategy
Forest → light → possibility → humanity. Keep the existing photography and star. An editorial home with readable project rows replaces redundant card discovery. Deep forest headings, luminous white surfaces, a restrained citrus accent, generous typography. Consistent compact navigation including a directly visible REWIND. Static destinations work without JavaScript.

Every new interaction answers a question: REWIND asks what changed and what persisted; the research walkthrough asks what may cross a scope boundary. Use native controls, live status text, stable layout and no remote inference. Existing evidence demo and Lenses do more valuable work than new decoration. Remove global pointer-follow effects rather than add spectacle. No scroll hijacking or required motion.

## Scientific/evidence posture
Explicit categories: empirical evidence, interpretation, design principle, theological interpretation, speculation. Primary sources: Sharma et al. sycophancy; LongMemEval; CIMemories; Fang et al. revised psychosocial study; NIST 2024 GenAI profile; Amershi et al. HCI guidelines. The revised Fang abstract differs from early reporting: no significant assigned-condition effects; voluntary heavier use is associated with worse outcomes. Do not turn this into a causal diagnosis. Benchmarks evaluate bounded tasks, not this architecture. Internal synthetic experiments provide no external validation here.
